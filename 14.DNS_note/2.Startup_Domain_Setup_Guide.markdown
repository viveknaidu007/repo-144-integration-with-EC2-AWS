# Startup Domain Setup Guide: End-to-End Steps After Buying a Domain

This guide outlines the essential steps for a startup that has purchased a domain (e.g., `theadtip.in`) to set up a website (`www`), email services (`vivek@theadtip.in`), Zoho Mail, Google Workspace, and subdomains. It also answers whether there's a limit to subdomains and provides a general process for all companies.

## Step 1: Understand Your Domain and DNS Provider
- **Domain Registrar**: Where you bought the domain (e.g., Squarespace, GoDaddy, Namecheap).
- **DNS Provider**: Manages DNS records. By default, your registrar (e.g., Squarespace) provides DNS management, but you can use third-party DNS providers like Cloudflare for better performance and security.
- **Action**: Log in to your registrar’s dashboard and locate the DNS management section (e.g., Squarespace’s "Domains" > "Manage DNS").

## Step 2: Set Up `www` Subdomain for Your Website
The `www.theadtip.in` subdomain directs users to your website.

### Steps:
1. **Access DNS Management**:
   - Log in to your registrar (e.g., Squarespace).
   - Navigate to DNS settings.
2. **Add Records for `www`**:
   - Create a **CNAME** record to alias `www` to your website host (e.g., Squarespace, AWS, or another hosting provider).
   - Example (if hosted on Squarespace):
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | CNAME | www | ext-cust.squarespace.com | 3600 | DNS Only |
   - If hosted elsewhere (e.g., AWS EC2 with IP `203.0.113.10`):
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | A | www | 203.0.113.10 | 3600 | DNS Only or Proxied |
3. **Configure Your Hosting Provider**:
   - In Squarespace, link the domain in the "Domains" settings.
   - For AWS, configure your server (e.g., Nginx) to handle `www.theadtip.in`.
4. **Test**: Visit `www.theadtip.in` after DNS propagation (1–48 hours).

## Step 3: Set Up Email (`vivek@theadtip.in`) with Zoho Mail
Zoho Mail provides professional email services using your domain.

### Steps:
1. **Sign Up for Zoho Mail**:
   - Go to `mail.zoho.com` and sign up for a free or paid plan.
   - Add your domain (`theadtip.in`) during setup.
2. **Verify Domain Ownership**:
   - Zoho provides a **TXT** record or CNAME to verify ownership.
   - Example TXT record:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | TXT | @ | zmverify.zoho.com | 3600 | DNS Only |
   - Add this in your DNS management panel.
3. **Set Up MX Records**:
   - Zoho provides MX records for email routing.
   - Example:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **Priority** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|--------------|---------|------------------|
     | MX | @ | mx.zoho.com | 10 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | mx2.zoho.com | 20 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | mx3.zoho.com | 50 | 3600 | DNS Only |
4. **Add SPF and DKIM for Email Security**:
   - Add a TXT record for SPF to prevent email spoofing:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | TXT | @ | v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all | 3600 | DNS Only |
   - Follow Zoho’s instructions to add a DKIM record (another TXT record).
5. **Create Email Accounts**:
   - In Zoho Mail admin panel, create accounts like `vivek@theadtip.in`.
6. **Test**: Send a test email to `vivek@theadtip.in` and verify receipt.

## Step 4: Set Up Google Workspace for Email and Google Login
Google Workspace provides email, Google Drive, and Google account integration (`theadtip.in` for Google login).

### Steps:
1. **Sign Up for Google Workspace**:
   - Go to `workspace.google.com` and choose a plan.
   - Add `theadtip.in` during setup.
2. **Verify Domain Ownership**:
   - Google provides a TXT record or other method (e.g., HTML file upload).
   - Example TXT record:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | TXT | @ | google-site-verification=abc123 | 3600 | DNS Only |
3. **Set Up MX Records**:
   - Replace Zoho MX records (if used) with Google’s:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **Priority** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|--------------|---------|------------------|
     | MX | @ | aspmx.l.google.com | 1 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | alt1.aspmx.l.google.com | 5 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | alt2.aspmx.l.google.com | 5 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | alt3.aspmx.l.google.com | 10 | 3600 | DNS Only |
     | MX | @ | alt4.aspmx.l.google.com | 10 | 3600 | DNS Only |
4. **Add SPF and DKIM**:
   - SPF TXT record:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | TXT | @ | v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all | 3600 | DNS Only |
   - Follow Google’s instructions for DKIM.
5. **Enable Google Login**:
   - Google Workspace allows users to log in with `vivek@theadtip.in` for Google services.
   - Configure in Google Admin Console under "Users."
6. **Test**: Log in to Gmail with `vivek@theadtip.in` and verify Google Drive access.

**Note**: You cannot use Zoho Mail and Google Workspace simultaneously for the same domain unless you use subdomains (e.g., `mail.theadtip.in` for Zoho, `@theadtip.in` for Google).

## Step 5: Adding Subdomains (e.g., `bot.theadtip.in`, `web.theadtip.in`)
Subdomains allow you to create distinct services under your domain.

### Steps:
1. **Access DNS Management**:
   - Log in to your registrar or DNS provider.
2. **Add an A or CNAME Record**:
   - For a server with IP (e.g., `203.0.113.10`):
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | A | bot | 203.0.113.10 | 3600 | DNS Only |
   - For aliasing to another domain:
     | **Type** | **Name** | **Content** | **TTL** | **Proxy Status** |
     |----------|----------|-------------|---------|------------------|
     | CNAME | web | www.theadtip.in | 3600 | DNS Only |
3. **Configure Server**:
   - Ensure your server handles requests for the subdomain (e.g., update Nginx or Apache config).
4. **Test**: Visit `bot.theadtip.in` or `web.theadtip.in` after propagation.

## Step 6: Are There Limits to Subdomains?
- **Answer**: There is **no strict limit** to the number of subdomains for most registrars and DNS providers.
- **Practical Considerations**:
  - **DNS Provider Limits**: Some providers (e.g., Cloudflare free plan) may cap total DNS records (e.g., 3,000 records). Each subdomain requires at least one record.
  - **Server Capacity**: Your server must handle traffic for all subdomains.
  - **Management Overhead**: Too many subdomains can complicate DNS and server management.
- **Examples**: You can create `bot.theadtip.in`, `web.theadtip.in`, `app.theadtip.in`, and more, as long as your DNS provider and server support them.

## General Process for All Companies
Every company should follow these steps after buying a domain:
1. **Choose a DNS Provider**:
   - Use registrar’s DNS or switch to Cloudflare/Google Cloud DNS for better features.
2. **Set Up Website**:
   - Add `www` and `@` (root domain) records pointing to your hosting provider.
3. **Set Up Email**:
   - Choose a provider (Zoho, Google Workspace, or Microsoft 365).
   - Add MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
4. **Add Subdomains**:
   - Create subdomains for services (e.g., `api`, `blog`, `shop`).
5. **Secure the Domain**:
   - Enable **DNSSEC** at your registrar.
   - Use a DNS provider with DDoS protection.
6. **Monitor and Backup**:
   - Regularly check DNS records for errors.
   - Export DNS settings for backup.
